# Market Research: Smart Glasses in Germany (`smart-glasses-de`)

## 1. Executive Summary
Germany represents Europe's largest industrial economy and a high-value B2B market for enterprise smart glasses (assisted reality / AR wearables), particularly across Industrie 4.0 manufacturing, automotive engineering, logistics, and remote field maintenance. However, consumer and workplace adoption in Germany is uniquely gated by strict data protection laws (GDPR/DSGVO), criminal privacy statutes (**StGB § 201**), and mandatory employee co-determination rights (**BetrVG § 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6**).

## 2. Market Landscape & Segments
* **Primary B2B Verticals:**
  * Automotive & Mechanical Engineering (*Maschinenbau*): Quality assurance, assembly guidance, digital twin overlays.
  * Logistics & Warehousing: Vision picking, hands-free barcode scanning.
  * Energy & Field Service: Remote expert troubleshooting ("See-What-I-See") to counter skilled labor shortages (*Fachkräftemangel*).
* **Key Competitors:** RealWear (Navigator 520), Vuzix (M400), Microsoft (HoloLens 2 legacy enterprise), XREAL / Rokid (lightweight tethered options).

## 3. Regulatory & Cultural Barriers (Critical Findings)
* **DSGVO / GDPR Art. 25 (Privacy by Design & Default):** Biometric data, bystander faces, and workplace video feeds require strict minimization, on-device redaction, and EU/German sovereign cloud hosting (ISO 27001 / BSI C5).
* **StGB § 201 (Confidentiality of the Spoken Word):** Unauthorized recording of non-public spoken words is a criminal offense in Germany. Smart glasses with microphones must provide unmistakable hardware-level visual and acoustic indicators whenever audio/video capture is active.
* **BetrVG § 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6 (Works Council / *Betriebsrat*):** Any technical device capable of monitoring employee behavior or performance requires prior formal agreement (*Betriebsvereinbarung*) with the company's Works Council. Without a "No Employee Surveillance" mode, enterprise deployments stall in procurement.
* **DIN EN 166 / PPE Certification:** Industrial buyers require impact protection and prescription-lens compatibility for shop-floor compliance.
