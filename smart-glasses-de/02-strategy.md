# Positioning Strategy: Smart Glasses for Germany (`smart-glasses-de`)

## 1. Core Positioning Statement
> **"Datenschutz mit System — Enterprise Smart Glasses engineered for German Industrie 4.0, featuring hardware-enforced privacy, zero employee surveillance, and Sovereign EU Cloud compliance."**

## 2. Strategic Pillars
1. **Hardware-Enforced Compliance (StGB § 201 & DSGVO):**
   * Physical camera shutter + unbypassable 360-degree crimson LED recording ring + subtle acoustic start/stop chime.
   * Real-time edge AI face and license-plate blurring before any frame leaves the device.
2. **Works Council Ready (*Betriebsrat-konform*):**
   * Dedicated "No-Tracking / Zero-Telemetry" enterprise firmware profile with pre-drafted *Betriebsvereinbarung* templates to cut procurement cycles by 50%.
3. **Industrial Ergonomics & Sovereignty:**
   * 68g lightweight frame, DIN EN 166 certified safety shield, hot-swappable batteries for 8-hour shifts, and 100% Frankfurt (DE) ISO 27001 / BSI C5 data residency.

## 3. Target Buyer Personas
* **Primary:** Head of Smart Factory / Digitalization (*Leiter Digitalisierung & Industrie 4.0*) at Mittelstand and DAX manufacturers.
* **Gatekeepers:** Data Protection Officer (*Datenschutzbeauftragter - DSB*) and Works Council Chair (*Betriebsratsvorsitzender*).
