# Marketing Copy Architecture (`smart-glasses-de`)

## 1. Hero Section (DE / EN)
* **DE Headline:** *Präzision im Blick. Datenschutz mit System.*
* **EN Subtitle:** *Enterprise Smart Glasses built for German Industrie 4.0 — engineered with hardware-enforced DSGVO & StGB § 201 privacy and full Betriebsrat approval readiness.*
* **Primary CTA:** *Pilotprojekt & Betriebsrat-Paket anfordern* (Request Pilot & Works Council Kit)
* **Secondary CTA:** *Hardware-Datenschutz testen* (Test Hardware Privacy)

## 2. Key Value Propositions
* **0% Verdeckte Aufnahmen (Zero Covert Capture):** Mechanical shutter, bright LED ring, and acoustic chime ensure complete transparency on the shop floor.
* **100% Deutsche Datensouveränität:** Hosted exclusively in Frankfurt am Main (ISO 27001 & BSI C5 certified) with edge-based PII blurring.
* **Sofort bereit für den Betriebsrat:** Delivered with a turnkey *Muster-Betriebsvereinbarung* proving zero individual performance tracking.

## 3. Compliance & Trust Guarantees
* Full compliance with **DSGVO Art. 25**, **StGB § 201**, **BetrVG § 87 Abs. 1 Nr. 6**, and **DIN EN 166**.
